# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until
they prove otherwise. Critique code, not people.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and should be specific: what breaks, under
  what input, and what you would do instead.
- When a maintainer says no, ask why once; if the answer holds, let it go.
- Keep review threads about the change. If a thread stops being about the code,
  move it to an issue or drop it.

## Not tolerated

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and
publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers at the address on the organization profile.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close
issues and pull requests, or block accounts, and will say which rule was broken.

This is a deliberately short document: it covers the behaviour that actually
shows up in a code-review project. The Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org) is the reference if you want a longer
statement.
